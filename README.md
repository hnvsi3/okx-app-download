## Maker and taker fees explained

A **maker** order adds liquidity to the order book. A **taker** order matches an existing order immediately.

In simple terms:

- A limit order that remains on the order book may be treated as a maker order.
- A market order that fills immediately is generally treated as a taker order.
- The final classification depends on how the order executes.
- The fee is calculated using the applicable rate for the product and account tier.

OKX explains that spot trading fees are calculated using the fee rate and the amount filled. The app also lets users view fee information for a trading pair and check their current fee tier.

The lowest percentage is not automatically the cheapest overall option. With instant buy, sell, or convert functions, the cost may be reflected in the quoted price or spread rather than displayed as a separate order-book commission. OKX notes that the quoted buy and sell prices can differ because of the spread and conversion mechanics.

Before confirming a transaction, compare:

- The displayed execution price
- The quoted spread
- The separate trading fee, if shown
- The network or withdrawal fee
- The amount you will actually receive
